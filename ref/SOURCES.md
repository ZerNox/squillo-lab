# Reference clips

Both clips are the first verse of "Amazing Grace" (John Newton, 1779; tune
"New Britain", 1829), cut from public-domain recordings on Wikimedia Commons.
The guitar was removed with Demucs (`htdemucs`, two stems, MIT licence; the
voice stem is kept), because the accompaniment threw the pitch tracker into
octave jumps. Then downmixed to mono, encoded at 96 kbit/s MP3 with a 0.8 s
fade-out. The separation leaves faint guitar traces and some artefacts.

"Polished" and "rough" name the role each clip plays in the comparison (a
trained professional soloist against a live amateur performance), not a
measured verdict on either singer.

Scored by the page (Chrome, 2026-10-24), vocal stems: pitch roughness 39 ¢
against 69 ¢, harmonics-to-noise 22.6 dB against 18.1 dB, scooped onsets 0.7
against 2.0 per 10 s. Before separation the guitar made the professional take
score worse on roughness (90 ¢ against 65 ¢). WebKit decodes to slightly
different numbers (roughness 26 ¢ against 78 ¢) with the same ordering.
