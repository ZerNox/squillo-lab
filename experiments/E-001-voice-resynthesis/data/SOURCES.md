The VocalSet files are scales sung on the vowel /a/, straight tone: no lyrics
and no melody from any work. Formant values in `voice.py`: Peterson and
Barney (1952), J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 24(2), 175–184, doi:10.1121/1.1906875
(means for men and women, /ɑ/); Klatt (1980), J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 67(3),
971–995, doi:10.1121/1.383940 (F4, F5 and bandwidth defaults). Both used as
synthesis parameters only.
